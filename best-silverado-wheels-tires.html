Bolt Pattern: 6x139.7

The Chevy Silverado 1500 uses a 6x139.7 bolt pattern. Any wheel you buy must match this exactly. Running the wrong bolt pattern is unsafe and will not fit.

Tire Size Guide

Stock size: Most Chevy Silverado 1500 trucks run 265/70R17 or 275/65R18 stock depending on trim.

With leveling kit (2–2.5"): Up to 285/70R17 or 275/65R18 without rubbing on most trims.

With full lift (3–4"): Up to 305/70R17 or 33" tires depending on lift and wheel offset.

AT vs MT — Which Tire?

All-Terrain (AT): Best for 80% road, 20% trail. Quiet enough for daily driving, capable on dirt, gravel, and light mud. Falken Wildpeak AT3W and Cooper Discoverer AT3 are the top picks.

Mud Terrain (MT): Built for serious mud, rocks, and off-road. Louder on pavement, faster wear. BF Goodrich KM3 is the benchmark.
