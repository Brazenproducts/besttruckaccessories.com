What to Know Before You Lift

Leveling kit vs full lift: A leveling kit raises the front 1.5–3" to match the rear — the factory builds in a nose-down rake for towing stability. A full lift raises all four corners and allows significantly larger tires.

Will I need a UCAs? For lifts under 2.5", OEM upper control arms are fine. Above that, aftermarket UCAs prevent ball joint binding and maintain alignment geometry.

Alignment: Any suspension lift requires an alignment after install. Budget $80–120.
