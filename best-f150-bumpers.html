Bumper Buying Guide

Steel vs aluminum: Steel is heavier but stronger and cheaper. Aluminum saves 30–50% weight but costs more. For serious off-road use, steel is the standard.

Winch compatibility: Most aftermarket steel bumpers include a winch plate. Confirm your winch's mounting bolt pattern (most use 4x4.5" or 4x6") before buying.

D-ring mounts: Look for integrated D-ring tabs — these are your recovery attachment points. 3/4" shackles fit most recovery situations.

Airbag compatibility: Critical. Any bumper must be confirmed compatible with your truck's front airbag sensors. Never run a bumper that blocks or repositions sensors without an adapter.
